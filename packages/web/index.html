Controls
    • Move the mouse to aim; click to lock the aim
    • 8-ball: the call follows your aim; to call another shot, lock the aim (click), then click a ball and a pocket
    • Hold the left button and drag toward you to draw the cue; release to shoot
    • Right-drag: orbit · Wheel: zoom · Middle-drag: pan
    • Ball in hand: drag the cue ball
    • ←/→ fine aim (Shift ×10) · W/A/S/D tip · R/F elevation
    • 1/2/3 views · G aim guide · Space skip replay · Esc cancel
